Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about construction costs in Pakistan.

How is construction cost per sqft calculated in Pakistan? +

Total cost is calculated as built-up area (in sqft) multiplied by the per-sqft rate for your chosen package. Built-up area includes covered space across all floors, but not open courtyards. The per-sqft rate depends on your city, package tier, and number of floors.

What is the difference between a Marla and a square foot? +

A Marla is a traditional land measurement unit used across Pakistan and India. In most cities, 1 Marla is approximately 225 sqft, and 1 Kanal equals 20 Marla (about 4,500 sqft). This calculator uses the standard 225 sqft conversion.

What does the "Basic" package include? +

The Basic package covers a complete, move-in ready home built with economy-grade materials — structure, plumbing, electrical, doors, windows, tiles and paint are all included, just using more budget-friendly local brands than the Standard, Premium, and Luxury tiers.

Why does construction cost vary between cities? +

Labour rates, material transport cost, and local market competition all vary by city. Karachi and Islamabad tend to have slightly higher rates due to labour demand and logistics, while smaller cities like Faisalabad or Multan are generally more economical.

How accurate is this estimate? +

This calculator gives a planning-stage estimate based on typical market rates. Actual cost can vary ±10-15% depending on soil conditions, design complexity, material price fluctuations, and site-specific factors. Always get a detailed quote from a contractor before finalising your budget.

What is the best time of year to start construction in Pakistan? +

October to March is generally ideal across most of Pakistan — dry weather supports better curing and material handling. Starting before the monsoon (June–September) helps ensure your structure and roofing are complete before heavy rains.

Can I switch packages after starting construction? +

Yes, but it's best to finalise your package before structural work begins. Switching finishing packages mid-way (e.g., from Standard to Premium) is usually possible without major rework, since finishing work happens after the structural shell is complete.
